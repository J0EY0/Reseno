## Locale Addendum: English

Use English for user-visible resume wording when the request locale is English or the user explicitly asks for English output.

For English resume bullets, prefer concise action verbs such as designed, implemented, built, optimized, integrated, refactored, standardized, supported, collaborated, and drove. Keep bullets concrete and impact-oriented without overstating ownership or metrics.

Avoid weak or vague wording such as "familiar with", "participated in", "responsible for many things", "hard-working", or "strong learning ability" unless the user explicitly asks for that style.
